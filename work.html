Selected Work
Amazon · Alexa Shopping

Product Q&A

Owned the product strategy for a conversational Q&A experience designed to answer real customer questions before purchase — not generic FAQs, but specific, contextual answers grounded in product data. The hardest part wasn't the AI; it was deciding, case by case, when an uncertain answer was worse than no answer at all. I worked closely with applied science and trust & safety to get that line right.

Amazon · Alexa

Generative Shopping Experiences

Shaped the product strategy for new generative AI shopping experiences on Alexa, at a point when LLM-powered shopping was still mostly a demo, not a product. My job was to turn genuinely impressive early capability into something dependable enough for customers to actually use — which meant a lot of unglamorous work on evaluation, edge cases, and knowing where to hold the line on launch readiness.

AWS

Global Technology

Partnered with global engineering and business teams to bring AWS technology and services into new markets. The technical work was the easy part — the real job was translating deep technical capability into something regional teams could adopt with confidence, across different regulatory environments, infrastructure realities, and customer expectations.

Speaker Bio

Joann Praise Emmanson is a Senior Technical Product Manager and entrepreneur whose career spans Amazon, AWS, and Alexa, alongside founding J'aduné Beauty, Pearl & Roses, and Brkeven. She holds an MBA from the W. P. Carey School of Business and a BS in Industrial Physics from Covenant University. She speaks on AI product leadership, entrepreneurship, and building multiple ventures at once.
